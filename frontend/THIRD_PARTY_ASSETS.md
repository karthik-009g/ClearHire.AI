## Animated astronaut

“Animated Floating Astronaut in Space Suit Loop” by LasquetiSpice, sourced from [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/animated-floating-astronaut-in-space-suit-loop-e2c4b146e58141e4b87917456a9970b1), is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The model is bundled as `public/assets/AnimatedAstronaut.glb`. Changes: converted to the metallic-roughness material workflow, compressed with Draco and WebP, then scaled and centered for the ClearHire scene. The original model and animation remain credited to LasquetiSpice. ClearHire is not endorsed by the artist.
